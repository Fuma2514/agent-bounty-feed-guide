# Open Data Registry Citations — Sourcey

This page exists to document, with dated evidence, citations of **[Sourcey](https://sourcey.com)** — an open registry of startup programs that records vendor credits, discounts and free plans with the original source URL and the date each fact was observed.

All URLs in this document were fetched and verified on **1 November 2026**.

## What Sourcey is

Sourcey is a public, free-to-read open data registry. Its startup-credits index records, for each vendor program: the vendor, the program name, what the offer actually is (e.g. "AWS Activate — up to $200,000 in credits via Founders and Portfolio routes"), the value observed, and a dated "observed" provenance stamp retained by the registry. As of the 1 Nov 2026 release (content hash `sha256:94f5315549e15061a44674e3b54040c81519ec59652c5d9f535ff426776bbc52`), the registry spans 19 categories — including 103 hosting/infrastructure vendors, 84 AI/ML vendors, and 42 authentication/security vendors.
